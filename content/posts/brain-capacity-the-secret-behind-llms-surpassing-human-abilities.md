Today’s LLMs far surpass human capabilities across a wide range of domains.

This naturally leads us to wonder: why can they perform better than humans?

It is because they possess “brain capacity” several orders of magnitude greater than that of humans.

People generally use IQ to measure how intelligent someone is. But there are two other important factors: brain capacity and ways of thinking.

Only by considering all three together can we truly assess a person’s intelligence.

At present, LLMs appear to fall short of the brightest humans in IQ and lag far behind humans in how they think. Yet their immense brain capacity compensates for both shortcomings, enabling them to exhibit remarkable intelligence.
